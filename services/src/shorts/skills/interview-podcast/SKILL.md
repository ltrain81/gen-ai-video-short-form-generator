Defaults, not limits: the user's explicit request always wins over anything below.
- Read the transcript with speaker labels; pick question → answer pairs that stand alone.
- Hook with the strongest answer line, then optionally show the question as a text item ("Q: ...").
- Cut on sentence boundaries; tighten pauses between speakers (< 0.4 s).
- Two speakers in one wide shot → layout "split" with both faces, or crop to the active speaker per segment
  (`analyze_framing` intent "keep the person speaking").
- Captions on with speaker-appropriate placement; preset "bold" or "karaoke" for social.
