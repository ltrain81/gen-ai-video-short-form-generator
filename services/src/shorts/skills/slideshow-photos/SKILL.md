---
name: slideshow-photos
description: Make a video from images, photos or document pages.
---
Defaults, not limits: the user's explicit request always wins over anything below.
- Use the images (and document pages) attached or found; `look` to order them into a story.
- Default 2–4 s per image (follow the user's pacing or a music beat if given); Ken Burns movement alternating direction; crossfade transitions (0.3–0.5 s).
- Music on a1; cut image changes to beats when a music track exists (`snap` "beat").
- Titles/captions from image captions or the user's synopsis.
