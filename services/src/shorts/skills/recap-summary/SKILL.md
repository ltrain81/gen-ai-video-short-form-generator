name: recap-summary
description: Condense a long video into a shorter summary (e.g. 1 h → 3 min, or 10 min → 60 s).
---
- Use chapters as the backbone; take the key sentence(s) per chapter (speech-led) or the defining shot (visual).
- Keep chronology. Add a short text title per chapter for long-form recaps.
- Optional voiceover script: if the user wants narration, write it and place it later when TTS is available;
  otherwise rely on original audio + captions.
