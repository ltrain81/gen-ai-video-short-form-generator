- Use the images (and document pages) attached or found; `look` to order them into a story.
- 2–4 s per image; Ken Burns movement alternating direction; crossfade transitions (0.3–0.5 s).
- Music on a1; cut image changes to beats when a music track exists (`snap` "beat").
- Titles/captions from image captions or the user's synopsis.
